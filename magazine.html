Meet the Birthday Girl

Where do I even start? 🙈

Rahina was born and raised in Damongo, in the Savannah Region — and she carries that origin with quiet pride. Standing tall at 5'10", with dark, smooth skin that seems to glow and is impossible to miss in any room,is a proud Muslim and beautiful hijabi. But it's not just her presence that draws people in — it's her voice. She speaks her mind freely, boldly, without apology, and somehow always says exactlywhat needs to be said.

She's currently studying Computer Engineering — a program that's anything but easy — and watching her push through it has been one of the proudest things to witness. There was a time I saw her hometown through a narrow lens, until she opened up and shared stories that completely changed how I saw where she came from, and honestly, how I saw her too.

She's a lover of books, a fearless experimenter in the kitchen, and somehow the only person whose jokes are funny because they're NOT FUNNY — though don't tell her that, she still thinks she's hilarious. She mothers everyone around her, pushes people to be braver, and once she sets her mind on something, nothing short of the universe itself can stop her.

  • Known for: speaking her mind boldly, no filter required
  • Superpower: making you feel loved and looked after, always
  • Signature move: telling a joke that isn't funny — and insisting it is
  • Loves: a good book, a new recipe, and getting exactly what she sets her mind to

How It All Started

How We Met

Honestly, I don’t even remember how we met, but I don’t regret meeting you one bit. The tallest girl I know😂. You’ve always made me feel so small and treated me like your child.😂 I remember you crying in class after school one day, and I asked you what was wrong. You told me what happened, which I honestly didn’t expect you to do, and funny enough, I had been through the exact same thing. It felt like we were sharing the same pain, and somehow, that moment brought us even closer. I’ve never been as open with anyone as I am with you. It’s so easy to tell you everything, sometimes without even having to say much, because you just get me. You know my ins and outs, and you’ve seen every version of me. And then there were the times we fought or stopped talking, and I made absolutely no effort to fix things because, honestly, my ego is taller than me😂. But somehow, you always fought to keep our friendship together. You never just let us fall apart, and I’ll always be grateful for that. I don’t know what life has in store for us, or where it’s going to take us, but I’m genuinely happy that I get to spend the rest of my life with you ❤️.

A Moment We'll Never Forget

I’ll always cherish every moment I spent in SHS with you. We practically did everything together—washed, bathed, ate together, shared our provisions. We would have even shared our panties if it came to it.😂 I’ll never forget any of it. From all the stories you used to tell me about the North, to the time Nustuga caught us in Estelle’s room, Earnestina’s gari🤣, our late-night eating in the dorm, or the time we stopped talking and literally everyone was trying to get us back together.😂 I don’t want to go too much into everything else, otherwise I’ll just end up repeating everything you already wrote in your birthday message to me. But honestly, I’d always cherish the times we spent together. Those memories will always have a special place in my heart. Maybe one day, years from now, we’ll sit down and tell your kids all these stories and laugh about how crazy we were.😂❤️

Where We Are Now

In uni, I know we both had different things going on, and honestly, we barely did much together. I also knew very well that we weren’t exactly happy about me doing things with someone else, and lowkey, it was funny to me🤣 because why would you really think anyone could replace you? Don’t even get me started because you’re always posting different people and writing about how much they mean to you but I none of them can compare to me.😉 So clearly, we both know where I stand.😂 I know we rarely see each other or even take snaps together, and that’s something I’m going to make a conscious effort to change. I want us to make more memories and actually have more moments to look back on. Sometimes, when your things come, you can be very annoying, ngl😭. I know it’s the same for me, but I try my best to overlook it and give you the space you need. At least even it’s better now.😂 But at the end of it all, no matter how busy life gets, how many people we meet, or how much changes around us, I know one thing will always stay the same—it’s always going to be you. Always. ❤️
